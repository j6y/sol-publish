> [!abstract] Angular Frequency ($\omega$)
> Rate of change of the [[Phase|phase]] ($\theta$) of a [[Periodic Signal|periodic signal]].
>
> $$ \omega = \frac{d\theta}{dt} $$
>
> Units: radian (rad) / second (s)

## Relations

| Parameter          | Equation            |
| ------------- | ------------------- |
| [[Frequency]] | $f = \omega / 2\pi$ |
| [[Period]]    | $T = 2\pi / \omega$ |
