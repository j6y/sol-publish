> [!abstract] Current Divider
> A [[Linear Circuit|linear circuit]] that outputs a [[Current|current]] that is a fraction of it's input current, determined by the ratio of [[Impedance|impedances]] (Z) in the circuit.
>
> $$ I\_{n} = I\_{in} \frac{Z\_{eq}}{Z\_{n}}$$
