## Setup

- Start with Aggressive preset

### Tyres

- toe - f: -0.15
- toe - r:  0.05
- camber: min
- caster: max
- psi: [[ACC - Tyre Tuning]]

### Electronics

- TC: default, 6 for Ferrari 296
- ABS: default
- ECUMap: 1
- TC2: off

### Mechanical Grip

- antiroll bar - f: max
- antiroll bar - r: min
- brake power: max
- brake bias: slider 25%
- steer ratio: default
- wheel rate: min
- bumpstop rate: min
- bumpstop range: slider 50%
- preload differential: min

### Dampers

- bump: min
- fast bump: min
- rebound: max
- fast rebound: max

### Aero

- ride height: default
- rear wing: max
- splitter: min
- brake ducts: [[ACC - Tyre Tuning]]
