> [!abstract] Power Factor (pf)
> Ratio of [[Real Power|real power]] (P) to [[Apparent Power|apparent power]] (|S|), representing the efficiency of power transmission.
>
> $$ \mathrm{pf} =  \frac{P}{|S|} = \cos \phi$$

Where,

- $\phi$: [[Power Angle|power angle]].
