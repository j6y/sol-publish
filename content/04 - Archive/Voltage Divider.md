> [!abstract] Voltage Divider
> A [[Linear Circuit|linear circuit]] that outputs a [[Voltage|voltage]] that is a fraction of it's input voltage, determined by the ratio of [[Impedance|impedances]] (Z) in the circuit.
>
> $$ V\_{n} = V\_{in} \frac{Z\_{n}}{Z\_{eq}}$$
