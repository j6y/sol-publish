> [!abstract] Voltage (V)
> [[Energy]] or work (W) required to move a unit of [[Electric Charge|electric charge]] (Q) from lower to higher electric potential.
>
> $$ V = \frac{dW}{dQ} $$
>
> units: volt (V) =  [[Energy|joule]] (J) / [[Electric Charge|coulomb]] (C)
> analogy: Water pressure between two points in pipe.
