> [!abstract] Metastable
> A state of **unstable equilibrium**, where a system can reside for an extended period. A sufficient disturbance (such as noise or an external trigger) may push the system towards a more stable, lower-energy state. Both the transition delay and final state are unpredictable.

- Electronic context: [[Setup Time|setup time]] or [[Hold Time|hold time]] violations in a [[Flip-Flop|flip-flop]] may lead the internal regenerative feedback loop into metastable state.
